# Third-party 3D assets

All third-party models in this directory are from [Kenney](https://kenney.nl/) and are released under the Creative Commons Zero (CC0) license.

Included packs:

- [Car Kit](https://kenney.nl/assets/car-kit) — vehicle bodies, wheels and debris;
- [Animated Characters: Survivors](https://kenney.nl/assets/animated-characters-survivors) — rigged character, run/idle animation and skins;
- [City Kit: Industrial](https://kenney.nl/assets/city-kit-industrial) — factories, tanks and chimneys;
- [City Kit: Commercial](https://kenney.nl/assets/city-kit-commercial) — commercial buildings and skyscrapers.

The original CC0 license text is stored in `KENNEY_CC0_LICENSE.txt`.
